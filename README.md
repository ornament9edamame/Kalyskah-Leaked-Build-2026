## 🕹️ About

Kalyskah is a dark fantasy third-person action RPG set in the world of Thulgatha. Play as Kalyskah Karnstein, an ancient vampire queen awakening from millennia of slumber to rebuild her army, hunt betrayal, and shape humanity’s fate through combat, blood-feeding, exploration, and mature story choices. This is a raw pre-release build that surfaced before the finished version was ready.

For players who want a mature, gothic vampire RPG with open-world exploration, real-time combat, character customization, and an unfinished underground-build atmosphere.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🧛 **Ancient Vampire** — Play as Kalyskah Karnstein, a powerful vampire queen returning from a millennia-long sleep.
- ⚔️ **Real-Time Combat** — Fight bandits, trolls, undead, animals, and other threats with dynamic melee combat.
- 🩸 **Blood Feeding** — Hunt enemies, bite their necks, and manage the weaknesses that come with vampirism.
- 🌒 **Dark Fantasy World** — Explore the open-world regions of Thulgatha, shaped by gothic fantasy and ancient catastrophe.
- 🗡️ **Vampiric Progression** — Develop attributes, skills, levels, and supernatural abilities as Kalyskah regains her power.
- 🎭 **Branching Choices** — Make moral decisions, build alliances, and influence Kalyskah’s relationship with humanity.
- 🎒 **Loot And Inventory** — Collect equipment and resources while exploring quests, dungeons, and the wider world.
- 🌦️ **Living Conditions** — Experience changing weather, a day-night cycle, thirst, and sunlight as active gameplay pressures.
